NASA data sources & scientific references

1. Catalogue of Manmade Material on the Moon

NASA History Program Office, dated 7-05-12. Integrated: Apollo landing coordinates; descent stage, ascent stage, central station and laser reflector coordinates; Surveyor, Ranger, Lunar Orbiter and LCROSS locations; which Apollo missions list an LRV or MET. Unknown values stay unknown. Not yet integrated: Soviet, Chinese, Indian, Japanese and ESA entries, and the long lists of small items.

Open the catalogue (PDF)

2. Mars Exploration Rovers Data Archive

NASA Planetary Data System, Geosciences Node. Integrated: the data set listing for Spirit and Opportunity (names, raw EDR or derived RDR type, links). Not integrated: individual data files, images or measurements. Instrument descriptions are general explanations written for this site, not text from the archive.
